# Laila Money Exchange · ไลลา มันนี่เอ็กเชนจ์

> Money exchange counter in Chiang Rai, Thailand, on Thai Wiwat Alley by the Night Bazaar. It buys and sells foreign cash for Thai baht. US dollars are priced by note size: 50–100, 5–20 and 1–2.

- Address: 48–49 Thai Wiwat Alley, Wiang, Mueang Chiang Rai 57000, Thailand
